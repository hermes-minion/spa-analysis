4. AI / HPC contracts and customers

Microsoft — the anchor contract

Sources [T1]: 10-K "AI Cloud Services Contracts"; 3-Nov-2025 release https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000114036125040072/ef20058139_ex99-1.htm; 13-Aug-2026 release https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000114036126032638/ef20080141_ex99-1.htm

  • Agreement date 2-Nov-2025, announced 3-Nov-2025. Dedicated GPU services at "Horizon" facilities, Childress, Texas, over a five-year average term.
  • Total contract value ≈ $9.7bn through 2031; four tranches (Horizon 1–4), each 50MW IT load (= 200MW IT total), NVIDIA GB300 GPUs.
  • 20% of each tranche's contract value payable before delivery, credited against service fees after the 24th month of the service term, pro rata.
  • GPU quantity and monthly payments "expected to be approximately equal across all four tranches."
  • Dell is the hardware supplier: purchase agreement dated 2-Nov-2025 with Dell Marketing L.P./Dell Canada Inc. for ~$5.8bn of GPUs and ancillary equipment; Partner Statement of Work is tri-party (IE US Hardware 3 Inc., Microsoft, Dell Canada). 10-K exhibit list items 10.6–10.8.
  • Horizon 1 delivered to and accepted by Microsoft in Aug-2026 (announced 13-Aug-2026); NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud status on GB300 NVL72 achieved at Horizon 1. Horizon 2–4 targeted for phased delivery in calendar Q4 2026, with contractual grace periods extending from mid-Q4 2026 to the beginning of Q2 2027 (10-K risk factors).
  • Implied average annual revenue [CALC]: $9.7bn / 5 ≈ $1.94bn/yr; the company itself used "$1.9bn average annual revenue under Microsoft contract" in the Q3 FY26 release notes.
  • Parent guarantees (10-K Note 23): hedge guarantees; a remarketing-right guarantee (if Microsoft validly terminates a funded tranche and no replacement customer is found, IREN Ltd guarantees the allocated debt/notes net of GPU sale proceeds plus any upfront amount owed to Microsoft); and a managed-services performance guarantee.

NVIDIA — customer and strategic partner

  • May-2026: five-year cloud services contract, ~$3.4bn TCV, "air-cooled Blackwell GPUs," deployed within 60MW of existing data centers at Childress, "targeting ramp from early 2027" (Q3 FY26 release, 7-May-2026) [T1]. 10-K confirms five-year term supporting "internal AI and research workloads."
  • 5GW strategic partnership to support deployment of up to 5GW of NVIDIA DSX-aligned AI infrastructure across IREN's pipeline (10-K; Q3 FY26 release).
  • Investment rights: NVIDIA can acquire up to 30,000,000 ordinary shares at $70.00, vesting in tranches based on GPU volume supplied, expiring 7-May-2031; grant-date fair value $793.4m, capitalised into GPU cost; "right to invest up to $2.1 billion ... subject to delivery of up to 600,000 GPUs" (10-K Note 24; Q3 FY26 release). This is the only disclosed upper bound on GPU purchase volume [T1].

9. Bear cases, short-seller and sceptic claims

No formal short-seller report on IREN was located in this review [NF]. The credible bear material is (a) the company's own risk factors and numbers, and (b) two reported sceptic episodes.

9.1 Structural / accounting bears (all T1, from filings) - Customer concentration: Microsoft ($9.7bn) + NVIDIA ($3.4bn) = "a substantial majority of our contracted revenue" (10-K). Microsoft's contract also carries termination/remarketing rights; the parent guarantees the allocated debt if a tranche is terminated and not re-let. - Losses and impairments: FY26 net loss $(702.6)m vs +$86.9m FY25; operating loss $(1,046.7)m; $638.8m of impairments in FY26 (Q4 alone $450.4m) from decommissioning mining hardware; loss on disposal $(24.9)m. - Adjusted EBITDA is flattered by exclusions: FY26 Adj. EBITDA $245.7m excludes $205.0m SBC, $638.8m impairments, and $417.7m D&A. Q4 Adj. EBITDA fell to $19.2m (14% margin) from $121.9m (65%) in Q4 FY25 — the AI cloud ramp has not yet replaced mining cash flow. - Cash flow quality: FY26 operating cash flow of $2,100.4m is almost entirely the $1,841.7m deferred-revenue prepayment; ex-prepayment CFO ≈ $259m against $4.33bn capex → underlying FCF ≈ −$4.07bn [CALC]. - Funding gap / dilution: FY27 capex guide $25–30bn vs $14bn of cash + committed GPU financing/prepayments; management targets ~$8bn more GPU financing/prepayments and expects the residual from data-center financing, operating cash flow and corporate debt and equity (FY26 deck p.22). FY26 share count already rose ~47%; the ATM has $3.5bn of unused capacity ($6.0bn program less $2.49bn used) before any new authorisation. - Execution risk: Horizon 2–4 (150MW IT) all targeted in calendar Q4 2026, with delivery grace periods only to early Q2 2027; the 10-K discloses prior NVIDIA-supply delays that pushed Horizon 1. 480MW gross must be commissioned in H2 2026 to hit the >$4bn ARR target, and ARR is an operating metric that "may be materially lower" than GAAP revenue. - Regulatory: ERCOT Batch Zero re-verification/audit ordered by the Texas Governor on 3-Aug-2026; 10-K explicitly flags possible energization delays or reduced energization levels at Texas projects (Childress, Sweetwater). - Governance: dual-class B shares (15 votes each) controlled by the co-CEOs; co-CEOs each received 9.1m RSUs in Jul-2026. Capital Brief (7-Jul-2026) reported shareholder/sceptic criticism of the co-CEO pay package [T2]. - Technology/obsolescence: GPUs depreciated over 5 years but the 10-K warns of "rapid technological change", shorter economic lives and residual values, and re-contracting risk where contract terms (3–5 years) are shorter than asset lives.
