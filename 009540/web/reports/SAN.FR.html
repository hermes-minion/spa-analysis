1. The setup

  • The re-rating is underway: +24% in a month. Q3 2026 (30-Oct) was a clear beat — revenue €15.23B (+15.7% YoY), business EPS €2.52, and Dupixent sales +34.8% — prompting a full-year guidance upgrade. The shares had been left for dead on Dupixent patent-cliff fears; the market is now marking up the durability of the franchise.
  • The valuation gap is the argument. Sanofi trades at 9.4x 2027E EPS and 8.0x EV/EBITDA, versus 12-14x for European large-cap pharma peers, with a 4.7% dividend yield. Even after the rally it is the cheapest quality pharma name in this batch.
  • The P&L optics are misleading. TTM GAAP EPS of €3.46 is depressed by a €0.66 loss in Q4 2025 (impairment/restructuring); FY2025 EPS was €6.37 and consensus 2026 adjusted EPS is ~€8.8-9.0. The trailing P/E of 25x is an artefact; the forward multiple is what matters.
  • Growth is no longer just Dupixent. Dupixent is ~40% of revenue and growing >30%, but the launch portfolio (rilzabrutinib, tolebrutinib, amlitelimab, Sarclisa, Beyfortus) is beginning to contribute. The company has also been an active acquirer (Blueprint Medicines, Vigil Neuroscience) to refill the pipeline.
  • Balance sheet is serviceable: cash €5.97B, debt €19.85B, net debt €13.9B, ~1.1x EBITDA. The dividend consumes ~€4.7B a year but is covered by €10B FCF.
  • Sentiment: price above the 50-DMA (€75.4) and 200-DMA (€76.9), 20% below the 5-year high. Only 43% institutional ownership (French large-cap norm is higher); the ADR (SNY) trades in the US.

2. Business and moat

Sanofi is a global pharma with three growth engines: Immunology (Dupixent, the largest), Vaccines (flu, Beyfortus RSV, meningitis) and Oncology/rare disease (Sarclisa, Cablivi, plus acquired assets). It also has a Consumer Healthcare carve-out in progress and a legacy diabetes/ cardiovascular portfolio in decline.

The moat is Dupixent's first-mover position in type-2 inflammation (atopic dermatitis, asthma, COPD, EoE) and Sanofi's vaccine franchise. The patent cliff is real — Dupixent loses exclusivity around 2031 — but the company has seven years to build the next leg. The market historically priced an immediate cliff; the Q3 beat shows the franchise still has pricing and volume runway.

Scenario math. Forward business EPS ≈ €9.3. Bear: Dupixent growth slows below 10% and the pipeline disappoints, EPS €8.8, multiple 8x → €70. Base: mid-single-digit growth, EPS €9.3, multiple 11x → €102. Bull: launch portfolio beats and Dupixent holds >15% growth, EPS €10.0, multiple 13x → €130. Probabilities 25/50/25 → ~€101 (+15%).

A 9.4x multiple for a pharma with a 4.7% yield and double-digit top-line growth is the anomaly. The bear case requires believing the pipeline is worthless and Dupixent collapses early.

5. Catalysts

  • Q4 2026 results (Feb 2027): FY2026 guidance delivery and 2027 outlook.
  • Dupixent COPD and paediatric expansion: label extensions.
  • Pipeline readouts: rilzabrutinib (ITP), tolebrutinib (nrSPMS), amlitelimab (atopic dermatitis) — three potential blockbusters.
  • Consumer Healthcare disposal: proceeds could fund buybacks or M&A.
  • Opella/consumer separation and capital allocation.
