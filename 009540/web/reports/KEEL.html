1. Business model & segments

Current model (management): "North American digital and energy infrastructure company that develops data centers and energy infrastructure to lease for HPC and AI workloads"; intends to lease to hyperscalers, cloud, AI companies under long-term contracts. Legacy Bitcoin mining is maintained in Canada only, "managed for cash generation" during conversion. [S2 Note 1; S1 Item 1]

Segment reporting — one segment only. As of 30-Jun-2026 Bitcoin Mining is the only reportable segment; HPC development and the PA energy-sales activity are not separate segments because the CODM does not review discrete results. Company says it "may identify a separate HPC reportable segment" only once HPC capacity is in service under executed customer contracts. [S2 Note 2 "Segment Reporting"; S2 Note 24] → There is no HPC/data-center revenue, and no segment-level revenue or gross margin for HPC or energy. Segment gross margin: NOT FOUND (not disclosed).

7. Bear case / credible sceptical evidence

  1. Pre-revenue HPC, no signed lease after ~18 months of pivot. 10-K admits zero HPC leases; Q2 release still only "active negotiations". Zero contracted MW against a marketed "2.2 GW pipeline" of which 1,513 MW (70%) is uncontracted expansion capacity. [S1; S2; S6]
  2. The advertised "pipeline" is mostly not de-risked. Only 648 MW is "secured" by a utility ESA; a further 123 MW of energised capacity (Panther Creek 60 + Scrubgrass 63) has no ESA at all and is being used for merchant energy sales (excluded from the 648 MW for that reason). None of the three priority sites is fully permitted (KBW; company says "nearing full permitting"). [S2; S6; S14]
  3. Legacy cash engine is being switched off. US mining ceased Apr/Jun-2026; Q2 revenue −50% YoY; gross margin −285%; Adjusted EBITDA −$23.7M in the quarter and −$40.4M in H1. Consolidated TTM net loss −$433.8M. [S2; S6]
  4. Balance-sheet leverage vs. an unproven business. $1.05B total debt principal, of which $1.046B is two convertible notes (vs. $1.35B market cap), $219.6M capex commitments, negative operating cash flow (−$117.6M H1). Both converts are struck at $6.86/$7.41 — 3.1x/3.4x the $2.19 share price, so equity conversion is remote and cash repayment/refinancing is the base case unless the stock triples. [S2; S10]
  5. Serial dilution. Shares +175% since 2022, +19.45% YoY; a further $458M convert in June-2026 raised dilution/financing-risk concerns (TipRanks). [S1; S2; S10; S11]
  6. Short interest 18.7% of shares out (115.5M shares, 15-Oct-2026) — the market is materially positioned against the story. [S13]
  7. Published bear framing: Seeking Alpha "Keel Infrastructure: Beware Delta-Of-Ruin Profile" (high-risk capital structure, regulatory headwinds); TipRanks "Why Keel Infrastructure's Big AI Bet Is Spooking Investors" (Sherbrooke land close slips to early 2027 and still needs provincial approval; funding/dilution fears). [S17; S11]
  8. Timeline slippage risk is documented, not hypothetical: Sherbrooke land purchase moved to Q1-2027 and remains conditional on Québec Ministry approval. [S2; S11]
  9. Governance/related-party: no related-party transaction identified in the reviewed filings. Ben Gagnon is CEO (quoted in Q2 release); Jonathan Mir is CFO. Board composition not reviewed. [S6]
  10. Auditor/controls: no material weakness disclosed in Q2-2026; disclosure controls concluded effective. FY2025 10-K audit opinion: NOT REVIEWED for this note. [S2 Item 4]
