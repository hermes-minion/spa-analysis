0. Classification (mandatory)

Profile B — profitable but cyclical. TTM revenue $1.13B, gross margin 70.8%, operating margin 32.2% and Q3 FCF $103.8M; net cash $775.6M with no funded debt. Margins are at a decade high, so mid-cycle — not trailing — earnings should set the multiple. Business model: machine-vision hardware (cameras, sensors, readers) plus software; factory automation and logistics. Metrics that matter: EV/EBITDA, P/E, EV/FCF, order growth and margin normalisation.

1. Executive summary

  • Best operating year in a decade; the stock is 31% off its June high. Q3 FY2026 revenue was a record $313.4M (+13.2%; +19% ex a one-time Q3 FY2025 channel benefit), GAAP operating margin 32.2%, adjusted EPS $0.53 (+61%) — the ninth straight quarter of margin expansion (Q3 release, 4 Nov 2026). FY2026 guidance was raised to $1.143–1.163B revenue (+16%) and adj EPS $1.68–1.72 (+67%). At $50.01 the market is debating whether 2026 is a new baseline or a cyclical peak.
  • The margin story is structural, not just cyclical. Adjusted EBITDA margin: 17.1% (FY2024) → 21.5% (FY2025) → ~31.5% guided FY2026, ~1,000bp above the old 25% exit target, from the salesforce rebuild, ~$22M of non-core exits, ~$35M of annualised cost cuts and a new pricing playbook (FY2025 release, 11 Feb 2026; Q3 call, 5 Nov 2026).
  • Growth is broader than at any point in a decade — four of five end markets grew double digits in Q3, logistics has grown double digits for 11 straight quarters, China +29% (+35% YTD), and semiconductor is only ~10% of revenue (Q3 call). The offset: 2027 consensus of +11% assumes no cycle break, and management flags logistics as having "the highest potential variability" into 2027 (Baird, 10 Nov 2026).
  • RealSense is Cognex's first true Physical-AI asset — and it is small. The $500M all-cash deal (announced 22 Sep 2026, closing Q4 2026) buys the leading 3D robotic-perception platform: $80–90M 2026 revenue (+>50%), >1M units shipped, ~20,000 developers, ~160 staff, 70+ patents, break-even, low-50s gross margin (8-K Ex-99.1). It adds ~7% to revenue and subtracts from group margin for 2–3 years, at 5.9x sales.
  • "AI machine vision" is real; the purity is not. ~100% of revenue is physical-world perception, but only 20–30% (centred ~25%) is revenue where a trained model does the work rather than a deterministic algorithm — see §3. The 10-K itself warns that open-source AI vision is lowering barriers to entry.
  • Balance sheet and dilution are best-in-class for this watchlist. $846M cash, no funded debt, 25% TTM FCF margin, FCF conversion 106%, SBC only 3.9% of revenue in 9M FY2026. Diluted shares +1.0% YoY; $99M bought back in Q1 FY2026, $151M in FY2025, $615M still authorised.
  • The debate is price. At $50.01: 29.4x FY2026E adj EPS, 24.9x FY2027E, 21.0x EV/EBITDA, 6.6x EV/sales — the low end of its own five-year forward P/E range (35–53x), a premium to every industrial peer except Keyence (12.2x EV/sales). Probability-weighted value $54.25 (+8.5%); bear $33, bull $74.

Verdict: NEUTRAL — conviction Medium. Fair value ~$55. A high-quality franchise in its best cycle in a decade, priced for the cycle to continue. I would turn constructive below ~$43 (≈21x FY2027E EPS, ~5x EV/sales, ~17x FY2026E EV/EBITDA), or on evidence 2027 growth is tracking above +12% with gross margin ≥70%.

2. Business overview

Cognex sells industrial machine vision: cameras, lenses and lighting, embedded vision systems, and the software that converts pixels into a pass/fail, a measurement or a robot coordinate. Management sizes its served market at ~$7B growing 10–11% through the cycle (Baird, 10 Nov 2026 — company estimate).

3. Physical AI exposure

Where it sits in the stack

SENSING COMPUTE DATA SOFTWARE SYSTEMS Cameras, optics, Embedded vision No proprietary VisionPro, In-Sight SLX; application- lighting (own systems; 3rd-party fleet or data firmware, OneVision, specific solutions plants CN/VN); SoCs (NVIDIA, asset — no REM- deep-learning tools (11.5% of revenue); RealSense depth Qualcomm); own ASIC equivalent flywheel RealSense SDK + custom ASIC in RealSense

Cognex is a sensing-and-perception company with a systems tail. It owns optics, illumination, the embedded vision engine and the software customers standardise on. It owns no data asset — nothing that compounds with installed base the way Mobileye's REM does. That is the structural difference between a 21x-EBITDA machine-vision franchise and a 100x autonomy platform.

What RealSense adds: custom depth ASICs, an SDK with ~20,000 active developers and >1.2M downloads, and a D585 "AI-native" depth camera with on-device inference at up to 30fps (M&A call, 22 Sep 2026). It is the only part of Cognex that maps cleanly onto the humanoid/AMR narrative.

4. Competitive position & moat

  • Largest pure-play machine-vision company: ~16% of its own $7B served-market estimate ($1.15B/$7B). Industry estimates put Cognex at 12–15% of the wider market and Keyence at 25–30% — unverified estimate, not disclosed.
  • >30,000 customers in >30 countries; management targets doubling the customer base in five years.
  • The installed base is the real asset: vision tools get specified into machine designs and stay for the life of the tool — a 5–10 year annuity of spares and upgrades.

What the numbers say

  1. Revenue has just cleared the 2021 peak ($1.04B → $837.6M in 2023 → $994.4M in 2025 → ~$1.15B in 2026E). Five-year CAGR ≈2% — the humbling context for 6.6x sales.
  2. The FY2024 gross-margin trough was mix, not lost pricing power. 71.8% (2023) → 66.9% (2025) reflected Moritex optics, logistics hardware mix and tariffs; it has recovered to 70.8% in 9M FY2026 on "faster-than-expected pricing execution" (Q3 release).
  3. Operating leverage is extraordinary and will halve. Q3 delivered ~100% incremental flow-through; FY2026 guides ~93%, and management explicitly warns of ~60% in FY2027 (Q3 call). Model 60%, not 100%.
  4. FCF quality is high: TTM $286M = 25.4% of revenue and 106% of net income; conversion has exceeded 100% for eight straight quarters. Capex ~1% of revenue.
  5. Two items distort the comps, both disclosed. Q3 FY2025 held a one-time $30M channel-partnership benefit; FY2026 carries ~$30M of headwinds (that plus ~$5M/quarter of divestitures). Ex-CP, Q3 grew 19% and FY2026 grows ~17% — the underlying rate is better than reported.
