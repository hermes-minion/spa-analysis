1. The setup

  • A conglomerate near its 5-year high, not a broken story. Sony is only 7% below its high and +7% YTD, with the shares above both the 50-DMA (¥3,899) and 200-DMA (¥3,564). The headline TTM net loss (-¥134B) is an accounting artefact of a ¥1.0T one-off charge in the Dec-2025 quarter, not operating deterioration.
  • Operations are healthy: TTM revenue ¥13.0T (+4.3%), operating income ¥1.96T (15.0% margin), EBITDA ¥2.3T, FCF ¥1.61T. Q2 FY2027 (Jul-Sep 2026) net income was +¥449.6B on revenue of ¥3.43T (+10.3% YoY).
  • The crown jewel is Imaging & Sensing Solutions: Sony supplies roughly half the world's CMOS image sensors for smartphones, cameras and automotive/industrial vision. That is the closest thing to a physical-AI monopoly in the portfolio — and the segment most exposed to smartphone unit saturation.
  • The other engines: Game & Network Services (PS5 install base, PSN, first-party studios), Music (recorded music + publishing, a streaming royalty machine), Pictures (film/TV, Crunchyroll anime), Entertainment Technology & Services (TVs, cameras, professional gear) and Financial Services (life insurance/banking in Japan, being partially separated).
  • Valuation is full for Japan. At 19.9x forward EPS and 2.0x sales, Sony trades at a premium to Japanese industrials but in line with its own history. The sum-of-parts (sensors, music, games, financial) can justify a higher value, but the conglomerate discount has persisted for a decade.
  • The stock's problem is not quality but upside: consensus expects only ~3% revenue growth over three years, and the big one-off charges have damaged confidence in disclosure.

2. Business and moat

Sony is a collection of leadership franchises. Image sensors (IMX) are a genuine technology moat: stacked CMOS, global shutter, automotive-grade qualification, and a customer list that includes every major smartphone OEM. Music owns publishing and recorded catalogs (a royalty annuity with pricing power). Games has the PS5 platform, digital store economics and IP (God of War, Spider-Man, The Last of Us). Pictures/Crunchyroll owns anime distribution. Financial services is a Japan-specific, regulated spread business.

The conglomerate structure dilutes the multiple: investors cannot own the sensor business without also owning insurance and film. Management has been simplifying (electronics spin-offs, financial-services separation), but slowly.

Scenario math. Forward EPS ≈ ¥219. Bear: smartphone sensors mature and games margins slip, EPS ¥195, multiple 15x → ¥2,925. Base: consensus delivered, EPS ¥225, multiple 20x → ¥4,500. Bull: sensor content growth in autos/robotics plus music/anime upside, EPS ¥245, multiple 23x → ¥5,635. Probabilities 25/50/25 → ~¥4,165 (-4%).

The stock is fairly valued. The bull case requires the sensor franchise to re-rate as an AI/robotics play; the bear case is a stronger yen and a smartphone replacement cycle that never arrives.

5. Catalysts

  • Q2 FY2027 results (early Nov 2026) — already reported: revenue ¥3.43T, net income ¥449.6B; the market is now watching H2 guidance.
  • Image-sensor demand from automotive/industrial vision and the iPhone cycle.
  • Financial-services separation — a potential value-unlock event.
  • Music catalog deals and anime (Crunchyroll) monetisation.
  • Buybacks: 2.2% share-count reduction YoY; Sony has a ¥ yen-denominated repurchase programme.
