2. Business Segment Analysis - company overview

What Boston Scientific does [FILED]

Boston Scientific (NYSE: BSX; founded 1979; ~59,000 employees) is a global medical-technology company. It develops, manufactures and markets devices across two reportable segments (as reported in 2026 filings):

MedSurg (Endoscopy, Urology, Neuromodulation businesses): - Endoscopy: GI diagnosis/treatment - resolution clips, biliary stents, SpyGlass single-use scopes (cholangioscopy), ERCP devices, AXIOS stent system, EXALT delivery systems, electrocautery. - Urology: stone management (LithoVue single-use ureteroscopes), BPH (rezum / GreenLight laser), incontinence (eCoin tibial neurostim - Valencia), prostate devices. - Neuromodulation: spinal cord stimulation (WaveWriter/Precision), peripheral nerve stimulation (Nalu), DBS (Vercise), sacral neuromodulation for bladder/bowel (InterStim).

Cardiovascular (sub-franchises: Cardiac Rhythm Management, Electrophysiology, Interventional Cardiology, Structural Heart, Peripheral Interventions): - CRM: pacemakers (INGEVITY), ICDs (EMBLEM), CRT, leadless pacemaker (in development: EMPOWER, H2-2027), remote monitoring (LATITUDE). - EP: FARAPULSE/FARAWAVE PFA catheters, OPAL HDx mapping, POLARx cryoablation, INGEVITY+ leads for EP. - Interventional Cardiology: coronary stents (SYNERGY), IVUS (AVVIGO), coronary IVL (SEISMIQ 4CE - investigational), complex PCI tools. - Structural Heart: WATCHMAN FLX/FLX Pro (LAA closure), ACURATE TAVR (being discontinued - 150bps headwind to 2026 organic growth per Q4-25 call), MiRus SIEGEL TAVR option (invested $1.5B for ~34% stake + exclusive option, Q2-26). - Peripheral Interventions: drug-coated balloons, SEISMIQ peripheral IVL (approved above/below knee), embolization (TruSelect), interventional oncology (TheraSphere). Plus pending Penumbra (thrombectomy) acquisition.

For each claimed advantage: what creates it / durability / replicability / direction of travel. [OPINION] unless labeled.

  1. Physician relationships & procedural know-how. Deep, decades-old training/proctoring networks (EP, IC, endoscopy, urology). Durability: moderate. Scouting/structuring = elite; integration = mixed (Axonics disruption; Silk Road manufacturing move; CEO "you can't afford turnover in that commercial team"). Direction: proven value creation history, now taking more concentrated bet.

Moat verdict [OPINION]: BSX's durable advantages are scale + category breadth + a value-creating M&A machine + very strong evidence/regulatory execution. The 2026 events showed none of these is decisive against a well-executed competitor in a high-interest market; the "wide moat" narrative is better described as a wide-but-contested moat, narrowing at the fastest-growing franchise until the 2027-28 launches prove otherwise.
