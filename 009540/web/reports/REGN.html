1. The setup

  • The best-performing large-cap biotech in the batch. REGN is +37% over 12 months and +23% YTD, versus a biotech sector that has been volatile. The re-rating is earnings-driven: Q3 2026 revenue was $5.12B (+36% YoY) and EPS $18.45 (+35%), and consensus now expects FY2026 revenue of $18.8B (+31%) and adjusted EPS of ~$68.75 (+55%).
  • Valuation has compressed even as the stock rose. At $955 the shares trade at 13.9x FY2026E adjusted EPS and 13.5x FY2027E (~$70.7), with $16.2B of net cash — roughly 17% of the market cap. Ex-cash, the operating business is at ~11x earnings.
  • The franchise is still EYLEA and Dupixent, but the mix is diversifying. Dupixent (with Sanofi) grew 34.8% in Q3; EYLEA HD is defending the retinal franchise against Vabysmo; Libtayo, oncology and the pipeline (baloncibart in POTS, a MASLD program with 62% liver-fat normalisation) are the optionality.
  • The market is paying for durability, not hyper-growth. FY2027 consensus growth is only +7% revenue and +3% EPS as EYLEA erosion and Dupixent's maturity bite. A 14x multiple on that is fair, not heroic; the upside comes from pipeline readouts and capital return.
  • Balance sheet: $16.2B net cash, no leverage, ROIC 16.3%, ROE 15.0%. Share count down 3.1% YoY. Altman 6.4, Piotroski 6.
  • Momentum is strong: price above the 50-DMA ($816) and 200-DMA ($747), 20% below the all-time high. Short interest 2.2%. The stock is extended short-term (+23% in a month) but not expensive.

2. Business and moat

Regeneron discovers and commercialises biologics. Revenue comes from Dupixent (atopic dermatitis, asthma, COPD, eosinophilic oesophagitis — shared economics with Sanofi), EYLEA/EYLEA HD (AMD, diabetic retinopathy), Libtayo (oncology), Praluent/Kevzara (legacy) and a growing oncology/immunology pipeline. It also has a large manufacturing footprint and a productive R&D engine (VelocImmune mouse platform).

The moat is the antibody-discovery platform and clinical execution; the risk is concentration. Dupixent is a mega-blockbuster with a 2031 patent cliff; EYLEA faces biosimilar/next-gen competition. Regeneron's answer is to reinvest the cash flow into oncology, immunology and metabolic disease — a strategy that has produced mixed results historically (see the 2022-24 pipeline disappointments).

5. Catalysts

  • MASLD/MASH data: the early trial showed 62% liver-fat normalisation; a Phase 2 readout is the highest-value pipeline event.
  • Baloncibart POTS Phase 2: positive data reported 12-Nov-2026; next step is Phase 3 design.
  • EYLEA HD vs Vabysmo share: quarterly retinal franchise trends.
  • Dupixent COPD/AD expansion and Sanofi's guidance: Sanofi raised FY2026 guidance on Dupixent strength (Q3 +34.8%).
  • Capital return: buybacks and the small dividend; $16B net cash is under-levered.
